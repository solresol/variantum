# AI4AS publication and experiment plan

Status: proposed by Greg to the co-authors on 2026-07-27; co-author agreement
and replies are pending.

This is the public-safe project-level record of the plan following the AI4AS
2026 talk. The verbatim email, co-author replies, research-output reporting and
HREC administration are retained separately in the private project
administration repository.

## Publication route

1. Reconcile the final hand-edited conference script
   (`into-the-parallage-ai4as-2026-conference-talk-v2.docx`) with the fuller
   paper draft (`../pdf/into-the-parallage-ai4as-2026-paper.md`).
2. Shape the paper for a computational-linguistics audience while preserving
   the ancient-text, translation-studies, human-oversight and pilot-limitations
   framing.
3. Circulate the paper to Shirley Chan, Vanessa Enriquez Raido and Greta Hawes
   for review and obtain all authors' approval before public submission.
4. Submit the approved paper to arXiv, with `cs.CL` as the intended primary
   category. Seek a computing endorser and use `cs.HC` as a secondary category
   if the final paper and arXiv classification support it.
5. Confirm whether AI4AS/DH2026 will publish proceedings, a special issue or an
   edited volume.
6. If there is a suitable archival AI4AS/DH2026 publication, use that route.
   Otherwise, submit first to *Digital Scholarship in the Humanities*, with
   *Digital Humanities Quarterly* as the next option.

The arXiv paper can provide a public, dated account of the hypotheses and
planned experiment. The final HREC-aligned design and analysis plan should also
be placed in a dedicated, timestamped preregistration before recruitment or
data collection; do not rely on a revisable arXiv version as the only
registration record.

## Research ethics and experiment

1. Target late August 2026 for preparing the HREC application, after the
   immediate PhD and employment work.
2. Ask Vanessa to liaise on the human-research-ethics process and confirm who
   should be the responsible Macquarie investigator.
3. Finalise the student-study design, recruitment, consent, data handling,
   exclusions, outcomes and analysis plan as part of the HREC package.
4. Wait for written ethics approval before recruiting students or collecting
   study data.
5. After approval, complete the operational plan, preregister the final
   protocol, prepare the study materials and run the student experiment.
